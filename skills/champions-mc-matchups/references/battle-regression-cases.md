# Battle decision regression cases

Anonymized behavioral scenarios, not logs or win
guarantees. Use the exact team, open sheets, HP, field and clock. Read
[M-C rules and tools](mc-rules-and-tools.md) for mechanics and actual MCP numerical
damage/Speed checks; unsupported benchmarks stay unverified, with no arithmetic
or direct-calculator substitute. An unresolved removal sequence needs the live tool's one actual MCP
`simulate_choices` outcome and an explicit deadline; broader offline cases can
use `simulate_turn`. Read [live battle lessons](live-battle-lessons.md)
for supporting evidence/counterexamples. Keep private access URLs, identifiers
and session logs private.

For denial/entry validation, begin with a recorded action lost to sleep or a
pre-action knockout, then present two sleeping actives and an actual selected
awake reserve. Before repeating attacks, compare the no-wake pair with that
reserve's eligible entry, both foes and its first useful action. No switch-turn
attack or reserve-availability proof; check preserving the reserve too.
Contrast delivered partner disruption, a known ineligible foe, and a necessary
trade earning a concrete next action. Permit those entries without requiring
both users to survive or inventing numerical safety.

A pass is a justified legal decision, useful first protection when actionable, and honest
attribution, not a prescribed move/string. In every case keep facts/hypotheses,
selected/sent/accepted/executed/defaulted outcomes and uncertainty separate.
Preserve applicable clock, authorization, ownership, freshness and reservation
limits. One result proves neither a roster replacement nor a different winner;
retain useful adaptation after losses. Test revised rules against countercases
before the next authorized queue and follow the play → conclude → update cycle.

## Counterexample exercises

- **Complete compact entry claims:** Give first-action/loss clauses ending
  mid-sentence, then complete but empty "if it survives/reassess later" claims.
  Before crediting future pressure, name the first useful action/window and the
  material loss continuation. Contrast delivered partner blocker removal with a
  usable beneficiary, a protected attacking line, a forced sole reserve, and a
  terminal attack with no reserves. Permit useful entries and purposeful trades
  without universal survival or whole-match proof; material numerical dependencies
  still need actual MCP checks.

- **Resolve the recipient before first protection:** Give a known immune/denied current recipient beside our partner Protect. Resolve sign/slot and partner payoff, then replace an ineffective pair before submission. Contrast an eligible recipient, useful attack beside Protect, and deliberate switch prediction: retain the latter only with stay-in failure and supported partner progress/purposeful trade. Credit timing to the first useful pair, not mere legality/later correction. Repeat the same reasoning for considered or revised pairs, changing only one recipient; evidence for the original pair certifies no other pair.

- **Expose the required answer beside Protect:** Give paired damage/denial stopping the needed attacker before it acts. Check the exposed user before crediting chip/next-turn protection, including revised pairs retaining that attack while its partner Protects. Reject pressure unless delivery or purposeful post-faint payoff is established; dropping the KO gate fixes nothing. Compare protecting that actor, actual denial, checked recipient/partner progress or explicit necessary gamble. Contrast delivered pressure preserving the remaining answer and a useful reserve-entry trade: both need not survive. Name the selected beneficiary and first useful action against both survivors; unselected/blocked last answers do not establish payoff.

- **Cover the required setup user:** Give support boosting its ally before that attacker can remove our control setter. Partner Protect covers no setter. Compare eligible Fake Out plus control, the foe's Protect branch and boosted next board; then add the other foe's usable Taunt, which invalidates attacker-only cover. Check actual priority protection/eligibility; permit ordinary pressure or checked trade when control is unnecessary.

- **Threat priority follows the route:** Give a needed weather setter under revealed coverage, weather active and opposing overwrite available.
