# Matchup and replay-review sources

Read and synthesized on **2026-10-02**. These are original summaries of the
decision frameworks. The VGC Guide chapters share one editorial source and use
historical examples; they do not establish current M-C usage or legality.

## Expert methodology, historical examples

- **[Team Preview](https://www.vgcguide.com/team-preview)** — Aaron Zheng, with
  an additional Aaron Traylor contribution. No date displayed. Choose a lead and
  reserves that function together against several plausible openings, and plan
  toward an endgame. Reassess selection after new information, even after a win.
- **[What is a Game Plan?](https://www.vgcguide.com/what-is-a-game-plan)** — Aaron
  Zheng. No date displayed. Identify objectives, the opponent's obstacles, and
  the resources needed to reach a winning position. Openings and recovery plans
  should support those objectives rather than form an inflexible move script.
- **[How to Analyze a Battle](https://www.vgcguide.com/how-to-analyze-a-battle)**
  — Aaron Zheng. No date displayed. Examine decisive positions, assumptions,
  selection, and alternative actions. Reassess unnecessary risk in wins as well
  as losses, and distinguish execution problems from reasons to change the team.

The old restricted Pokemon, Dynamax examples, and EV assumptions in these
chapters are not M-C recommendations. Adapt the reasoning and verify all battle
details through the Champions rules and tools. Do not turn the guides' emphasis
on player agency into a claim that every unlucky loss could have been prevented.
