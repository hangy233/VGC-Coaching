# Bounded turn planning and delivery checks

Use fresh supplied facts and actual MCP research to resolve the useful complete
legal pair before extended comparison. Apply the delivery
contract below to the current forcing plan; it adds no separate live checklist,
research round or reporting step. The agent supplies the objective, plausible
opposing pairs and final choice.
Carry the selected front/back jobs into that objective. When chip leaves the
blocker intact, a required resource may be spent, or an entry/control window
changes, compare the next useful pair after an attack-plus-positioning line with
the immediate attack pair. Use the [preview and two-turn procedure](proactive-win-plans.md#choose-the-route-then-create-pressure);
reaching a position without a usable next action is not its payoff.

Before labeling a pair independent, use one bounded scan: **resulting recipients /
field / forms -> reactive effects and priority eligibility -> each needed actor's
delivery -> delivered partner progress and remaining answer**. A selected form or
field change, or a switch-in ability, triggers this same scan before crediting the
attack window. This orders the existing checks; it adds no separate read, model
response or research round before first protection.

## Resolve required-actor delivery and recovery

### Identify material dependencies

Name the required attacker, setter or pivot, preserved resources and next
obstacle in the compact plan. Before crediting its action, scan **both foes** for an
eligible earlier hit or denial against **each needed actor and later answer**.
Apply this to every considered or revised pair, including useful alternatives;
research about one actor clears neither the other actor nor its stopping foe.
Test the actual payoff: if an actor cannot act or a named resource faints, what
useful delivered progress and remaining answer survive?
After those changes, check the resulting exposure even without an explicit
survival claim, especially when a preserved actor supplies the only immediate
progress beside Protect, a pivot or denial. With two attackers, apply it to each
actor the plan actually needs; independent useful chip can remain independent,
and two selected attacks alone supply no recovery.
A selector does not make its actor necessary. Before labeling a pair independent
pressure, use the compact loss test: **actor denied -> delivered partner progress
-> remaining answer or usable entry**. Apply it separately to each needed actor;
missing payoff leaves the dependency unresolved. Missing or withheld calculator
rows, including an empty incoming-risk list, supply no all-clear: scan both
foes' known coverage and denial, then apply this loss test before calling chip
beside partner Protect independent. Calling it a necessary gamble
also names why the available covered alternatives leave no plausible route; the
label alone cannot discharge a check. Keep this in the same plan, not another live reporting step.

After an observed denial or pre-action KO, begin the next choice with its cause:
what changed so the needed action or remaining answer can now contribute? Recheck
both foes before crediting another attack, including after replacement or an
item/ability change. Carry the actual early sequence into the next preview
against that same opponent: affected actor, the compatible paired targets,
execution/denial order and missing cover. A final plan or generic move-order note
does not replace it. If the provided context omits that sequence, disclose the
missing history; this instruction does not make a runner store or deliver it.
Use the available retained events or compact set history without a routine full
history read or new live research round.
After an observed paired KO, a different partner's Protect/Detect still covers
neither hit into the same needed actor. Keep that compatible paired-target branch
until current eligibility, actual cover or the delivered-trade route changes;
also allow the opponent to adapt, redirect or choose a different action. The
previous outcome proves no certain repeat or numerical benchmark.
Partner Protect, calling the action chip, or deleting a
removal claim does not resolve that cause. Name actual delivered partner progress
and the remaining answer's first useful action. Preserve a real delivered trade
or known ineligible threat as a countercase; do not require every ally to live.

When an active's progress needs unverified waking or recovery from denial, first
compare the current pair with that progress absent. Before repeating disabled
attacks, compare an actual selected awake reserve's eligible entry and first
action against both foes; a switch grants no entry-turn attack. Check weather,
protection, trapping and the cost to the staying partner. Reserve availability
alone proves no continuation, and entering is not compulsory: a necessary wake
gamble remains possible when covered alternatives leave no plausible route.
Keep this comparison in the same bounded choice; numerical dependencies still
need the existing actual MCP checks.

Before crediting paired pressure, check a current foe's Fake Out or other
before-action denial against current protection and eligibility, including when
both allies attack. Pair plausible denial of a needed actor with the other foe's
progress, then apply the loss test above. Do not credit an already prevented
action merely because its denier is removed later. For plausible Encore,
re-read the last **executed** move and current priority-protection holder on the
execution board; a newly selected attack need not be what resolves. If denial
leaves neither useful delivered progress nor a usable answer or entry, calling
the attack chip does not make the pair independent. Keep this in the same bounded
choice. Generic exposure alone triggers no research; retain ordinary independent
chip and purposeful partner-loss trades when delivered progress preserves a
usable continuation, without requiring every actor to survive.

After an observed Protect erases the pair, recovery restores its target while
spending a contributor, or setup keeps advancing, begin with the failed
conversion: **what does this pair change about the next heal, boost or attack?**
Name its useful delivered progress through the compatible defensive action plus
the other foe's attack or recovery. A different target, extra support, a named
reserve or "no removal required" alone supplies no change. Recheck drops/status,
actual entry/trap eligibility and the remaining answer's first useful action;
our healing alone does not break their cycle. Compare a concrete disruption,
target, control or resource change with both replies. Retain ordinary useful
chip, repetition and purposeful trades when that progress leaves a usable
continuation; a sole foe's Protect or an ineligible partner is a different cost.
Keep this within the same bounded choice and material-check contract, not a new
research quota. See the [conversion plan](proactive-win-plans.md#break-screens-and-recovery-backed-setup).

An exposed setup action, entry needing survival through both plausible incoming
actions, or required attack through a concrete plausibly eligible denial or
support-plus-attack route is provisional while that dependency is unresolved,
including beside partner Protect or a pivot. Identify the route from bound board
facts, not an automatic tactical warning. Generic exposure alone requires no
research; current protection or known ineligibility can resolve it. Move access,
typing, a risk label or narrower damage wording supplies neither a knockout nor
the missing continuation. Ordinary useful chip, purposeful trades and necessary
gambles retain their direct path.

If keeping opposing control absent requires stopping its setter, resolve that
route or plan for the effect being established. When both sides can reverse the
same global effect, such as Trick Room, start from its current state and include
the opposing setter's eligible action. Check denial/removal or name the useful
continuation if control is cancelled. A warning or attempted action proves no
continuation or execution; known ineligibility needs no invented branch.

### Resolve recipients and entry

As the final semantic check, match each named intended move to the selected
actor's exact current legal row, then its slot, target and Mega flag. Read runtime
legality even when the imported set lists moves in another order. If the stated
move and authored slot disagree, resolve the complete pair before commit against
the same fresh request; do not invent a replacement command, permission, timer
extension or extra acknowledgment round. Attribute the exact submitted slot and
observed execution honestly: disagreement in model intent is not evidence the
compiler substituted a different move. A later request change still requires
fresh legality and preservation of any existing send reservation.

Independently map each action's sign/slot to its recipient in every considered
or revised pair. Recheck priority, eligibility, field,
immunity, denial and relevant stat-drop/item/ability triggers after plausible
switches, pivots, redirection, Mega or replacement into the targeted slot.
Distinguish current form/ability from the selected transformation's resulting
exposure. Resolve entry-triggered copied abilities before crediting priority
denial or protection; a registered reserve and the possible copy remain
hypotheses until observed. A possible secondary drop is a branch, not certainty.
A selectable action is not useful protection.
For an immune current recipient, follow the source-bound
[recipient-read or ineffective-action contract](#read-current-recipients);
a prediction needs the expected recipient, stay-in failure and useful partner
progress/trade, and proves no opposing choice.

Check reactive stat effects on recipients on both sides, including plausible
opposing ally-targeted support. Resolve the recipient's resulting form/ability
and the support's order before the required-actor and removal tests: a nominal
drop can strengthen that ally, and priority protection on our side does not deny
their ally-targeted action. Use the changed stats in material checks rather than
an unboosted benchmark. Contrast support aimed at our protected side and an
ordinary recipient whose ability does not reverse the drop; neither gains the
same boost. When a damaging move has a possible opposing stat drop, carry the
boosted foe's plausible attack/control and the no-drop branch into the
delivery/remaining-answer test. Compare a useful different
recipient, single-target or type-stable pressure, protection or denial when the
reaction breaks the payoff. Retain the attack when necessary removal, delivered
partner progress or a purposeful trade justifies that risk; no blanket spread or
stat-drop ban. Other consequential item/ability triggers use the same ordering.
Use the existing actual MCP checks when the resulting numerical dependency is
material; the trigger alone calls for neither invented calculations nor a new
routine research round.

Before crediting field-dependent priority or ordinary attack delivery, put a known available
opposing setter's plausible switch ahead of the attacks in the initial scan:
**resulting field/recipients -> reactions -> each needed actor's delivery -> useful
failed-action payoff**. This includes ordinary attacks whose actor may lose its
window after weather changes order; it needs no priority move or terrain attack.
Re-evaluate earlier move-order evidence under the changed conditions. Calling
the attack chip or removing a KO claim does not resolve that denied-actor branch.
A current field icon or empty current-type card does not settle that
branch; a registered unconfirmed reserve remains a hypothesis. Compare staying in
too. Successful field activation establishes that field, not either actor's
survival or attack delivery: the other foe's eligible action still matters.
Before calling a setter entry a renewal, compare the field before entry with
the actual setting event. Identical active weather or terrain retains its observed
age; entry alone starts no new duration. A new activation after expiry or an
overwrite is a different branch; see the [field-duration check](live-battle-lessons.md).
Revise from facts or use the existing material checks; retain a purposeful trade
when delivered progress leaves the needed answer. Keep this in the same bounded
scan and attached plan, without an extra read or research round.

Before our switch, establish the current trap holder/form/ability, usable reserve,
escape eligibility and latest safe escape. An uncertain request flag proves no
executable switch. Name the entrant's first action window before crediting an
awake reserve as pressure: it cannot attack on the switch turn. Carry the
no-wake/denial comparison above into that entry window, including both foes and
preserving the needed reserve. A necessary entry or purposeful trade remains
available with a concrete beneficiary and next useful action; an exposure
disclaimer alone supplies no continuation.
Before crediting a compact entry payoff, finish its first useful action/window
and material loss-continuation clauses within the field limits. Name delivered
partner progress and the usable remaining answer if losing the entrant breaks
the route. "If it survives", "reassess later" or a cut-off clause alone supplies
no cover or purposeful loss payoff; shorten complete claims instead of cutting
their continuation. This adds no optional-metadata requirement or survival
guarantee.
Check both foes' plausible actions and combined pressure into
the entrant, including required survival and remaining pending attacks after a
forced mid-turn entry. A not-yet-evolved stone holder enters in base form; an
already evolved reserve keeps its actual form. Resistance/immunity to one hit
does not establish two-foe safety. Check denial as well as damage: earlier Throat
Chop can stop sound moves despite resistance/Fake Out immunity, and
[Psychic Noise can stop drains](mc-rules-and-tools.md#psychic-noise-can-deny-a-draining-attack).
An escape pivot must execute before the deadline through Fake Out, Protect,
Encore, KO or invalid-target branches; verify the switch and counter clearance.
An absent trapper or actually legal escape changes the branch, so impose no
blanket pivot ban. A necessary trade may remain useful without claiming safe entry.

### Check required delivery

Resolve a material dependency by revising from known facts, establishing
[useful failed-action recovery](#recover-failed-delivery), or asking the one material delivery/survival question through actual
`simulate_choices` with complete authored own/opposing pairs. Inspect attempts,
denials, native effects, ordered actions and pending boundaries. Add survival when needed:
acting before fainting and surviving are different goals. Verify the required
order/control window, surviving pair and next useful action. A material item or
ability change can unlock moves while invalidating the old order: check the
changed order through actual MCP before crediting the required attack. Check both foes,
including support strengthening an incoming hit. Partner Protect, its pivot or
a reserve's later ability does not cover the required actor. An already known
denial can justify immediate revision or separately useful failed-action recovery;
no calculation is needed merely to repeat it.

Verify consequential contact, form and ability claims from format-correct tool
results or pinned mechanics evidence; a move name or memory is insufficient.
Keep that factual question separate from full current-state reconstruction.
In particular, after a proven transformation, a null current-ability field can
withhold the numerical screen even when the resulting form's intrinsic ability
is available from the pinned format-correct tool or Dex. Retrieve that static
fact and, where supported, use it as an explicitly labeled numerical hypothesis.
Do not overwrite the observed field or equate intrinsic ability with verified
current ability: copying, replacement and suppression may still be unresolved.
Retain those alternatives when they change the calculation or plan. A static
lookup does not reconstruct missing PP/lifecycle or establish paired delivery.
Classify a check as unattempted, not exposed by the loaded authoring contract,
known unsupported from retained evidence, or actually refused with its returned
reason/receipt. Do not report a model's unattempted reconstruction concern as a
native refusal, or infer that static mechanics are unknown from a combined-state
limitation. Required numerical work still uses actual MCP, shared research limits
and original deadline rules; this distinction adds no standalone live round trip.
If a needed row remains unavailable, carry its threat as unresolved, rather than
infer safety from the screen's omissions. Compare covered protection or entry
with a purposeful trade and its remaining answer; an actually ineligible threat
or a delivered trade may justify attacking. Mere incoming exposure does not
force Protect, a speculative ability override, or a universal survival check.

Anticipate eligible revealed item removal before exposing a required holder.
Surviving the hit does not preserve a plan that needs its Speed item, protection
or recovery. Compare the useful pair and next action with that item removed,
including any unlocked moves and changed order; protect, switch legally or apply
useful pressure when that loss breaks the route. Accepting removal can still be
a purposeful trade when the item is no longer needed or the alternative is worse.
Use existing material MCP checks, not another routine research round.

If partner disruption enables the attack, resolve eligibility after plausible
switch/field/recipient changes before crediting protection; compare the uncovered
hit. An eligible target remains a different branch, so a field-dependent failure
does not ban the support move. For a conditional attack beside support/Protect,
check eligibility against the leading defense/withdrawal and the other foe's
progress. Applied support cannot deliver an ally's attack if that ally faints
first. Keep an existing field separate from one actually established by the action.

A required removal with an unresolved paired-sequence dependency needs the single
actual MCP simulation allowed by the [live research budget](#choose-the-smallest-useful-check)
before commit, with target and deadline explicit. Scalar damage or Speed results
do not establish an unresolved paired sequence. If removal must precede retaliation, inspect that deadline and
the surviving-foe branch before crediting drain healing, support or a field change
as protection. Reuse only matching actual MCP evidence; unavailable numerical
benchmarks remain unverified.

### Recover failed delivery

Carry observed/simulated pre-move KO or denial into every revised pair and
considered alternative. The same exposed attack beside partner Protect is not useful
chip or an independently useful alternative merely because its KO label was
removed. Retained evidence remains conditional and does not erase the failed
response.
Compare protection of the required actor, a checked recipient, eligible actual
denial and useful partner progress, or establish a purposeful post-faint payoff:
the actual selected usable reserve/active beneficiary, earned removal/control/
entry, first useful action against both survivors, and remaining answer.
For that first action, recheck protection and order lost with the departing
resource, including newly eligible priority after an opposing reentry. Naming
the reserve or calling chip conditional supplies no answer to paired denial.
An unselected reserve or unusable entry proves no payoff. A switching reserve
cannot attack on its entry turn. A deliberate trade need not preserve both users.

Before spending a needed actor in a revised pair, check the decisive
follow-up when needed to justify that trade. This bounded dependency check does
not require proving every future opposing choice. If unsupported or too late,
choose a viable independently useful alternative without the unchecked removal/
delivery/conversion dependency. A forced choice or explicitly necessary finishing
gamble remains available: state its failure, payoff and unverified limit rather
than claiming safe delivery or guaranteed removal.

### Preserve the later window

If payoff needs a resource later, resolve its concrete survival/denial dependency,
choose a covered alternative or identify the necessary gamble. Narrower wording
cannot erase that answer; a risk label proves neither preservation nor purposeful
trade. If independent pressure remains, state that narrower payoff and leave
preservation conditional. Resource uniqueness means one available provider,
not automatic indispensability; current protection needs no proof of every
optional attack. Read forms and possible switch/Mega field changes before reusing
order. If setter and closer are separated, name their feasible shared attacking
board and when renewal can resolve. A setter on the roster grants no protection
before renewal or after expiration; needed later beneficiaries must survive/act.

Unsupported, late, refused or stale checks remain unresolved. Samples establish
no reliability across untested branches/seeds; answer routes are conditional,
not universal counters, probabilities or opponent intent. Read unavailable
coverage and field-level assumptions. A returned sample establishes neither
tactical confirmation nor actual execution. Submit or revise promptly without
restarting broad ranking; a useful final pair without an unresolved dependency
goes direct. Optional notes or calculations must not delay submission. A justified double or trade remains available.


## Use bounded briefs and retained evidence

Start from the complete current board and legal menu already supplied. Optional
qualitative summaries or old reports cannot replace it or justify delaying a
useful pair. Reuse actual compatible calculation receipts; a model note is a
claim, not a receipt or observed execution. Missing rows remain missing.

Preserve source bindings, original input/result references and explicit omitted
coverage in retained evidence. A smaller presentation must remain lossless under
its declared encoding; unknown mappings require the full source, never guessed
defaults. A stored complete response does not prove complete model-visible
delivery. No summary is a new calculation or current-state authority.

## Read control and reconstruction cues

Read current source/deadline/legality before acting. Preserve uncertain-send
reservations and reconcile them; a fresh request does not authorize a duplicate.
Selection, send, acceptance, execution, default and inactivity stay distinct.

The simulator assembles known facts from native history. Missing lifecycle, PP,
hidden counters, copied state or a receive gap is a reconstruction issue, not a
ban on an otherwise clickable action. A conditional native world must identify
its selected defaults and unknowns. Exact known HP, boosts, items, moves and
history must not be overwritten to force a result.

A recurring diagnostic remains relevant until its proof changes; a new request
alone repairs no history gap. Do not retry an identical blocked question merely
to use the clock. When a smaller scalar question actually answers the decision,
ask it within the existing budget, without claiming paired delivery from it.
If a required sequence remains uncertain, choose an independently useful line
or state the necessary gamble and actual remaining answer.

## Read current recipients

Check both actions against the actual recipients, including immunity, ability,
field, form and plausible entry/redirection. A useful partner attack does not
supply pressure from an ineffective move. Missing metadata is no all-clear.

A deliberate switch/form prediction needs its expected recipient, current-target
failure and useful partner payoff. An intentional ineffective action, such as
forced input or purposeful stalling, needs an explicit purpose and no claim of
current damage. Neither prediction nor acknowledgment proves usefulness,
opponent intent or delivery. Do not automatically retarget authored actions.

## Keep preview intent with the submitted four

Retain the front-two task, both back-two jobs and entry windows, resource,
plausible reply and next change condition beside the explicit four/order.
Keep the actual selected order distinct from the intended plan; neither establishes
acceptance or a checked later route. Use the current final response schema rather
than an invented preview metadata tool or separate delaying note.

## Keep one active plan

Keep `planUpdate` short: objective, needed resource, current route, meaningful
opposing reply, unresolved risk and next change condition. Carry intent after a
request changes, then recheck it against fresh facts; old actions, targets,
bindings and receipts provide no new authority. Shorten complete claims rather
than cutting their loss-continuation clause.

Inspect durable private turns/decisions for between-set review. Bounded in-memory
history or a compact previous-game summary can omit the early cause; disclose
that omission instead of asserting the failed route was resolved.

## Schedule only material work

### Choose the smallest useful check

The battle model decides whether research changes its choice. Use actual MCP
`calculate_damage` for a damage/survival benchmark and `calculate_speed` for
numerical order; include the relevant actors in one supported Speed request.
Known immunity or denial needs no repeated calculation. Ordinary chip, a safe
Protect or an already justified switch does not require a simulator merely
because a new turn arrived.

Use supplied base priorities, current own reported Speed and prior observed
order as facts, not an effective-Speed ranking. Recheck items, boosts, status,
abilities and control changes before carrying an old relation forward.
Before a simulation call, inspect each hypothesized move's target class as well
as our clickable menu. Omit chosen targets for spread, self and field actions;
retain legal targets for targeted actions. Native base target metadata is not
the opponent's current menu: form-dependent targeting and conditional recipient
changes can differ. Do not blanket-rewrite a hypothesis from the base class.
Resolve that distinction from known mechanics/state or label the uncertainty;
native validation remains authoritative. A rejected research hypothesis is no
evidence that our live legal pair or the move's mechanics are unsupported.

Live research has a combined maximum of **three calculator calls** per battle
turn (`calculate_damage`, `calculate_damage_range`, `calculate_speed`) and **one
simulated outcome**. These are ceilings, not targets. Reuse identical compatible
results; prioritize the question most likely to change the pair. The limit is
shared with frozen waiting-time work. A replacement, reissued request or retry
within the same battle turn does not grant another allowance. Internal numerical
research must not bypass the calculator cap. A failed or uncertain backend call
does not refund a reservation; an invalid input rejected before dispatch is not
a completed calculation. Preserve the original clock and final-choice reserve.

Spend calculator capacity on the immediate tactical questions first. Live
simulation uses only compatible cached historical spread checks; it starts no
new automatic inference calculations. Unchecked profiles remain labeled
hypotheses, not history-validated investments. Use remaining capacity for an
explicit damage, Speed or spread question only when its answer could change the
pair. A simulation returning successfully does not establish that its hidden
spread was checked. Detailed offline inference retains its separate contract.

Use simulation only when known facts and scalar checks leave a consequential
sequence unresolved: actor delivery through both foes, interacting attacks or
support, entry/exit effects, redirection, or a needed removal before retaliation.
Choose one complete own/opposing pair and one labeled representative world/seed.
Explicit replacement continuations may finish that same trajectory; they do not
authorize extra branches. Read the compact end board, ordered consequential
events, turn-completion/pending boundary and uncertainty labels. Do not request
automatic alternative outcomes or interpret the single sample as an exhaustive
range, probability or guarantee. A paused replacement is not a finished turn.

Once the budget is spent, decide from the evidence actually obtained and known
facts. Change to a useful plan without the unresolved dependency when possible;
otherwise state the necessary gamble. Do not invent numerical work, silently
reset the budget, or spend the remaining clock repairing optional research.
Detailed multi-outcome review belongs between games or sets, outside live limits.

### Use the current choice card

Use the current model-tool schemas and actual fresh menu. The tool client adds
private source bindings; the model supplies tactical actions and inputs only.
Resolve move name, target class and slot before a call. Structural input refusal
is different from a native mechanics failure or a legal live-choice refusal.

A completed simulation may still be conditional or paused at replacement.
Include only the requesting sides in a continuation, omit the other side and
prefer stable switch identity. Numeric slots refer to that boundary's native
menu, not the initial party order. A blocked/completed pivot creates no choice.
Do not resimulate a different pair after the one outcome has been reserved.
